Data Deletion Instructions

Surf N Slides Meta application · effective 6 November 2026

What data the App holds

The Surf N Slides App is an internal tool that publishes Surf N Slides' own content to its own Instagram account, @surfnslide.uae. It does not offer Facebook or Instagram Login to the public. It does not store personal data about other people: it keeps only publishing records and totals (such as like and comment counts) for the Surf N Slides account. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How to request deletion

  1. Email surfnslides@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Include your name, your Facebook or Instagram username (if relevant), and a description of the data you want deleted.
  3. We confirm receipt and tell you what, if anything, the App holds about you. We delete any such data from the App's records and confirm by email when it is done. We aim to complete requests within 30 days.

Removing the App's access

If you ever granted this App access to a Facebook or Instagram account, you can remove that access at any time:

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy › Settings › Apps and websites › select "Surf N Slides" › Remove.
  • Instagram: Settings › Website permissions (Apps and websites) › select "Surf N Slides" › Remove.
